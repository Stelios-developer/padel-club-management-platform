Padel Club. A full-stack sports management platform, built around a real booking workflow.

Members explore the club and manage their games from a clean, Greek-language interface.

Secure sign-in leads to membership eligibility, court types and clear pricing before booking.

The local AI assistant turns a natural Greek request into live court availability. Here: tomorrow after six, indoors, for ninety minutes and two players.

The suggestion opens a confirmation form with the matching date, duration and price. The member confirms, and the backend saves the reservation.

The same reservation is available on mobile, with readable cards and clear actions.

Policy answers link to approved club sources. The assistant remains a prototype, with clarification and unknown responses for requests it cannot support.

Booking events deliver notifications asynchronously through Rabbit M Q and a transactional outbox.

A separate reception workspace supports the club's schedule and operations. This view uses isolated demo data.

Behind the interface: Spring Boot services, a Spring Cloud gateway, Keycloak authentication, and service-owned PostgreSQL databases. The AI uses Ollama and pgvector.

Local verification includes one hundred forty AI regression cases and a full two hundred forty-three browser-check run. Results and limitations are documented.

A working product, backed by reproducible engineering evidence. Ready for a code review and technical discussion.
